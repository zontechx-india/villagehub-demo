Guided flows

  1. Sign up as a new userMobile number → OTP → your name → empty home → Create a service.
  2. Create a chit groupOne-page setup with collapsible sections and a live settlement preview. Saved as a draft as you go.
  3. Run a live auctionTwo-minute auction with other bidders → approve the result → send the payout.
  4. Collect payments (organiser)Verify an agent's cash entry, record cash for an overdue member, reverse with a reason.
  5. Pay your dues (member)Your ledger in another chit. Pay round 3 by UPI and watch it confirm.
  6. Scheme calculatorRound-by-round simulation of a chit scheme.
  7. Temple collection & receiptPick a family → amount and method → receipt. Void and reissue it.
  8. Join a group with a codeEnter KWC-2026 → organiser approves → accept the rules.
